Everyday purchases.
Ongoing possibility.

Ongoing Fundraiser

For nonprofits, schools, PTOs, churches, clubs, community organizations, and sports teams.

It’s a free fundraising program that gives eligible organizations their own fundraising link. Supporters shop for everyday wellness products through that link, and the organization can earn on qualifying purchases and qualifying reorders.

  • No cost to start
  • Earn 20% on initial sales
  • Earn 10% on reorders
  • No upfront inventory
  • No products to keep on hand
  • Support from me every step of the way

Unlike a traditional fundraiser that ends when the campaign ends, qualifying repeat customer orders can continue generating fundraising revenue.
